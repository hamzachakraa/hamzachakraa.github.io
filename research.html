Research

Research interests

Aerial robotics

Taking coordination methods out of simulation and onto real drones in the field.

Multi-robot task allocation

Deciding which robot does what, and in which order, for missions with many tasks.

Decentralized coordination

Consensus and gossip-based algorithms that work without a central planner.

Swarm guidance

Guidance laws that steer fleets of drones over evolving areas such as fire fronts.

Multi-agent reinforcement learning

Learning cooperative policies for heterogeneous teams under partial observability.

Active perception

Moving sensors to see what matters: smoke, fire and changing environments.

Applications Exploration and navigation · Interception · Inspection and monitoring
